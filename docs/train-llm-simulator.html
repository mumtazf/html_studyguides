Decision Simulator

Train an LLM

You're the ML engineer. Make one call at each stage of the pre-training pipeline. Every choice trades off capability, training stability, and your compute budget — and carries a real-world dollar cost you can estimate as you go. Change your mind anytime with Back. Get to the finish line with a base model worth building on.

Use it to lead the discussion

  • Before revealing each tradeoff, have the group guess the dollar cost and type it in. How close were they to the reference?
  • Play once optimizing purely for capability, then replay on a tight budget using Back to change calls. What did you sacrifice?
  • Which single stage moved the total spend the most, and was it worth it?
  • Notice the recurring tension: nearly every capability gain costs compute or dollars or risks stability. Where is that trade sharpest?
