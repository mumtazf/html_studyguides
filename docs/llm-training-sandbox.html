LLM Training Trade-offs

Think of training an LLM like building a brain and sending it to read a library. How would you balance brain size vs reading volume?

1. Pick a Strategy Persona
🦙

Compact Brain + Giant Library

Llama 3 Style

Build a smaller brain (8B params), but make it read 15 Trillion tokens. High upfront training time, but fast & cheap to chat with daily.

⚖️

Balanced Brain & Library

Chinchilla Compute-Optimal

Scale brain size and reading list together (~70B params on 1.4T tokens). Maximum smarts per training dollar spent.

🐘

Giant Brain + Quick Skim

Undertrained Giant

Build a massive brain (175B params), but only read 300 Billion tokens. Very expensive to run, leaving empty brain capacity.

2. Adjust the Setup
🧠 Model Brain Size 8B Parameters
Compact (8B) Medium (70B) Giant (175B)
📚 Library Reading Volume 15.0T Tokens
Light (0.3T) Medium (1.4T) Massive (15T)
🧼 Book Quality
⚡ User Chat Speed & Cost Fast & Cheap
🧠 Overall Smarts / Intelligence High
💰 Pre-training GPU Bill Heavy Upfront
Why do this? Over-training a small brain takes more training time, but makes the final model extremely fast and cheap to serve to millions of chat users!

💬 Discussion Questions for Your Group

  • Serving vs Training: If you plan to serve 100 Million users a day, why is a smaller overtrained model (Llama 8B) better than a huge undertrained model?
  • Data Quality: What happens when you feed an LLM "dirty/duplicate" web text versus clean curated books?
  • Hardware Memory: Why is it so hard to fit a 175B parameter brain on a single GPU?
